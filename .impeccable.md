## Design Context

### Users
- **Nurses**: Ward station, shared tablet, gloves, interruptions. Jobs: vitals, drug charts, respond to alerts. Emotion: calm, efficient
- **Doctors/Clinical Officers**: Consultation room, laptop, patient present. Jobs: Dx support, orders, notes, AI second opinion. Emotion: confident, clear
- **Receptionists**: Front desk, queue, power flickers, touchscreen. Jobs: registration, payments, SHA/NHIF claims. Emotion: fast, reassuring
- **Lab/Pharmacy Techs**: Lab counter, desktop/tablet. Jobs: results entry, stock alerts, dispensing. Emotion: accurate, unambiguous
- **Administrators**: Office, laptop. Jobs: revenue, utilization, compliance dashboards. Emotion: in control, informed

### Brand Personality
**Voice**: Trusted. Pragmatic. Human.
**Tone**: A calm co-pilot that respects the clinician's time and the patient's life.
**Anti-references**: Legacy EMRs like Epic 2015, ChatGPT-style whimsy, banking apps with popups
**References**: Stripe Dashboard (data density), Linear (speed), M-Pesa for Business (offline-first + tap targets)

### Aesthetic Direction
**Visual tone**: Minimal-clinical with warm accents. Whitespace + clear hierarchy + one primary brand color.
**Theme**: Light default, dark mode supported for night shifts + battery saving.
**Colors**: Deep teal or medical blue for primary actions (trust). Saturated red/orange for critical/urgent only. High-contrast neutrals #111 on #FFF or #F7F7F7.
**Typography**: System fonts first for performance. Clear numerals for doses. 16px base size minimum.

### Design Principles
1. **One hand, one thumb**: Works while standing, holding a chart, or wearing gloves
2. **3-tap rule**: Any common task finishes in 3 taps or less
3. **Explain the AI**: Every AI suggestion shows reason + source + undo
4. **Urgent is sacred**: Critical alerts use color + sound + haptics. Everything else is calm
5. **Failsafe over fancy**: If network dies mid-dose entry, data is saved locally

### Accessibility Requirements
- **WCAG**: 2.2 AA minimum. AAA for drug doses, allergies, critical alerts. 4.5:1 contrast, 7:1 for vitals
- **Interaction**: 44px minimum tap targets. No hover-only actions. Keyboard shortcuts for clinicians
- **Motion**: All animations <200ms. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
- **Screen Readers**: Full ARIA, logical tab order, phonetic drug names
- **Language**: English + Kiswahili toggle. Voice input/output. Icons support low literacy
- **Connectivity**: Offline-first. Optimistic UI. Clear "syncing" and "offline" states. Never lose data
